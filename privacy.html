Privacy Policy — up_short (Sao Lại Thế)

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Who uses this app

up_short is a private tool used only by the owner of the "Sao Lại Thế" channel to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It has no other users.

2. Data we access

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives:

  • basic account information (display name) — to confirm the correct account before posting;
  • permission to upload and publish videos to that account.

The app does not access followers, messages, or any other user's data.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. Nothing is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. The app does not sell, share, or analyze data.

4. Deleting data

The owner can revoke access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the "Sao Lại Thế" page on Facebook.
